Private by architecture

Your memories stay under your control.

Memories are stored in Fondary’s protected local repository by default. Personal iCloud sync is optional, off by default, and uses your private CloudKit database under your Apple account.

Fondary operates no memory-sync server, includes no advertising SDK, and does not sell memory content. Voice recognition requires Apple’s on-device support.
